My Love,

Heyy babyyyyyy ❤️

You made it!! I’m so, so happy for you. 🥹❤️ Who would’ve believed that the same angry bird I met in 200 level would be standing here today as little Miss Graduate? Time really flew.

I’ve watched you through every phase. I’ve seen you crash out, cry, dance, laugh till your stomach hurt, doubt yourself, almost quit, pick yourself back up, and keep going anyway. I’ve seen you scared, I’ve seen you brave, and through it all, I’ve watched you grow into such an incredible woman.

I know today, as you sign out, you're probably trying to hold back tears of joy because the journey hasn’t been easy. You've been through so much, but somehow, every obstacle that came your way only made you stronger. You never stopped showing up for yourself, even on the days when it was the hardest.

I still remember how you got into FUTA. 😂 You were chasing something completely different and didn't even know you've gotten admission. Looking back now, it's almost funny because life had bigger plans for you. And honestly, that's one of the many things I admire about you. You're incredibly smart, beautiful, elegant, disciplined, laser-focused, and one of the most hardworking people I know. None of this happened by luck, you’ve earned every single bit of it.

Lately I've been thinking about how the girl I met back then isn't the same woman I'm looking at today.

I remember meeting a girl who was scared, constantly overthinking, carrying so much on her shoulders, and at times looked so depressed by everything life was throwing at her. There were moments you questioned yourself, moments when you couldn't see just how amazing you truly were. But look at you now. You're no longer that scared girl. You've become such a confident, graceful, resilient, loving, and inspiring woman. The way you carry yourself now, the way you believe in yourself, the way you pursue your dreams with so much confidence... it honestly makes my heart so full because I know the journey it took to get here.

You've become someone who doesn't just dream anymore, you make things happen. Iwo Odindin CEO HappyFeet and StyleSavvy, General Director Penguin Square Inc.. You're building enterprises, creating opportunities for yourself, chasing your vision, and inspiring people around you without even realizing it. Every time I see what you're building, I'm reminded that you're destined for greatness.

"You've become someone who doesn't just dream anymore...
you make things happen."

What's even more beautiful is that you've already accomplished things that many people our age haven’t even imagined doing yet. While some are still trying to figure out what direction to take, you've already taken bold steps, built systems you're proud of, created something with your own hands, and continue to push yourself to become even better. That isn't luck, it's discipline, courage, consistency, and God's grace.

Today isn't just about signing a shirt or taking pictures. It's proof of countless sleepless nights, assignments, tests, exams, sacrifices, disappointments, prayers, and moments where you chose not to give up. This is the reward for years of consistency, resilience, and faith.

I hope when you look around today, you don't just see the celebration, I genuinely hope you see how far you've come. I hope you're proud of yourself because I promise you, I'm beyond proud of you. Watching you become the woman you are today has been one of the greatest privileges of my life.

This is only the beginning, baby. There are so many more wins waiting for you, and I genuinely can't wait to stand beside you through every single one of them. Be it your next business milestone, your career, or all the dreams you've written down and prayed over, I'll always be your biggest fan, cheering you on every step of the way.

"This is only the beginning, baby."

Never forget this: your graduation is something to celebrate, but it isn't what defines you. What defines you is your heart, your resilience, your kindness, your ambition, and your willingness to keep going no matter how hard life gets. Those are the things that make me admire you the most.

Thank you for inspiring me with your strength, your determination, and your heart. Thank you for being you. Never stop believing in yourself the way I believe in you.

Congratulations, my love. ❤️ You did it. You earned this. Go and celebrate because today belongs to you.

I love you more than words can ever explain, and I'm so excited to see what God has in store for you next.

"I love you more than words can ever explain. ❤️"

Happy Sign-Out, my beautiful graduate. 🤍🎓
